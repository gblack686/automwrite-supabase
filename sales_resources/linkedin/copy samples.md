# LinkedIn Message Templates Documentation

## Purpose and Usage

These LinkedIn message templates are designed for outreach to different professional segments in the financial advisory industry. Each template is tailored for a specific audience segment and serves a particular goal in building a professional network.

## Template Categories

| Category | Target Audience | Primary Goal |
|----------|----------------|--------------|
| **Business Page Viewers** | Prospects who viewed company page | Convert passive interest into connection |
| **UK IFA/Directors/Advisers** | Financial advisors in leadership positions | Build industry network with decision makers |
| **Industry Professionals** | Regulatory/tech professionals in adjacent sectors | Create strategic relationships outside direct prospects |

## Customization Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `{name}` | Recipient's first name | "Sarah" |
| `[specific area]` | Customizable text based on prospect's focus area | "AI Strategy in advice" |

## Best Practices

1. **Personalization**: Always customize bracketed/variable sections for each recipient
2. **Brevity**: Keep messages concise (under 300 characters)  
3. **Clarity**: State your role and company clearly
4. **Value**: Emphasize connection benefits without direct selling
5. **Follow-up**: Plan for 2-3 follow-ups after connection acceptance

## Response Rates

- **Business Page Viewer Template**: ~25-30% acceptance rate
- **UK IFA Template**: ~15-20% acceptance rate
- **Industry Professional Template**: ~20-25% acceptance rate

## Integration with Buyer Personas

| Template | Best Matched Personas |
|----------|----------------------|
| Business Page Viewers | All personas (universal application) |
| UK IFA/Director/Adviser | Independent Adviser Emma, Efficiency-Oriented Ethan, Growth-Oriented Olivia |
| Industry Professional | Scaling Firm Sam, Wealth-Delegator William, Adviser Network Founder Alastair |
