# Email Copy Samples Documentation

## Field Definitions

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `id` | Unique identifier for the email template |
| `subject` | Email subject line |
| `body` | Full email body content |
| `target_personas` | List of buyer personas this email is designed to resonate with |
| `metrics` | Performance and classification metrics |

### Metrics Fields

| Metric | Description | Possible Values |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| `funnel_stage` | Position in the sales funnel | "Top of funnel", "Middle of funnel", "Follow-up", "Re-engagement" |
| `effectiveness_rating` | Estimated effectiveness on a scale of 1-10 | 1-10 (10 being highest) |
| `complexity` | Message complexity level | "Very Low", "Low", "Medium", "High" |
| `notes` | Additional context and usage guidance | Text |

## Funnel Stages Explained

1. **Top of funnel**: Initial outreach to new prospects
2. **Middle of funnel**: Communication with prospects who have shown interest
3. **Follow-up**: Subsequent messages to prospects who haven't responded
4. **Re-engagement**: Attempts to restart conversation with dormant prospects

## Effectiveness Rating Guide

- **9-10**: High-performing emails with proven results
- **7-8**: Solid performers that work well for targeted audiences
- **5-6**: Average effectiveness that may need customization
- **1-4**: Lower performers that should be used selectively

## Targeting by Persona Level

- **Level 1-2** (Independent Adviser Emma, Cost-Conscious Chris): Focus on simplicity, ROI, and ease of adoption
- **Level 3** (Efficiency-Oriented Ethan, Growth-Oriented Olivia): Emphasize scaling and technological advantages
- **Level 4-5** (Scaling Firm Sam, Wealth-Delegator William, Adviser Network Founder Alastair): Highlight enterprise features, compliance benefits, and team productivity
