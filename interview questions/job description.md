# MaintainX GTM Engineer Role

MaintainX is the world's leading Asset and Work Intelligence platform for industrial and frontline environments. We are a modern IoT-enabled cloud-based tool for reliability, safety, and operations on physical equipment and facilities. MaintainX powers operational excellence for 10,000+ businesses including Duracell, Univar Solutions Inc., Titan America, McDonald's, Brenntag, Cintas, Xylem, and Shell.

We've raised $104 million in venture capital following a recent Series C funding round led by Bain Capital Ventures, Bessemer Ventures, August Capital, Amity Ventures, Ridge Ventures as well as CEO's from GE, Twilio, Coupa, Toast and PagerDuty.

## The Opportunity

MaintainX is seeking a forward-thinking individual to revolutionize our go-to-market engine and refine how we scale pipeline generation and deal velocity. In this high-impact role, you will leverage cutting-edge AI tools like Clay, Zapier, and GPT-powered platforms to build automation systems that drive pipeline, outperforming the traditional GTM sales model. Your mission will include transforming how accounts are sourced, tiered, and prioritized, creating AI-driven workflows for personalized messaging, scaling marketing and sales content creation, as well as innovating on new concepts aimed at increasing efficiency and productivity throughout the sales funnel. By 2026, your work will set the foundation for shifting traditional GTM functions to focus solely on human-centric tasks by leveraging innovative automation.

This role is ideal for a technical individual passionate about leveraging data, AI, and automation to innovate, while also growing their personal brand in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

## What you'll do:

*   Build and optimize data enrichment systems using tools like Clay to enhance lead generation, segmentation, and campaign personalization for maximum impact.
*   Develop automated contact sourcing workflows to create precise, scalable, and high-quality prospect lists.
*   Design and implement AI-driven solutions, including email sequencing, call preparation, and content creation tools, to enhance sales team efficiency and effectiveness.
*   Leverage new technology to streamline account mapping processes, providing sellers with actionable insights into account structures, opportunities, and decision-making pathways.
*   Develop solutions to map corporate hierarchies and multi-location structures, improving visibility into organizational relationships and enabling strategic account planning.
*   Measure and analyze the performance of programs and team efforts, using data-driven insights to identify successes, areas for improvement, and optimization opportunities.

*   Familiarity with data processing pipelines, ETL systems, and API integrations to automate data workflows.
