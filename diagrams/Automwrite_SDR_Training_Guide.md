## The Financial Advice Landscape (UK)

### A) Financial Advisers

- Provide advice on investment, pensions, protection, taxes, retirement strategies.
- Regulated by the FCA, responsible for suitable, compliant, documented advice.
- Qualifications:
  - **DipPFS** (Diploma in Financial Planning)
  - **DipFA** (Diploma for Financial Advisers)
- Level 4 qualifications required under FCA’s RDR rules.

### B) Paraplanners

- Support with financial research and report writing.
- Draft suitability reports, cash flow models, and investment recommendations.

### C) Admin/Compliance Staff

- Handle onboarding, documentation, and ensure audit trails are followed.
