# Tiered Outbound Campaign Strategy: EATT Event Follow-Up

## Campaign Overview
- **Campaign Name**: EATT 2025 Delegate Outreach
- **Target List**: 50 companies from EATT Delegate List
- **Duration**: 6-week campaign (10 companies per tier per week)
- **Channels**: Email + LinkedIn
- **Sequence Length**: 5-15 days per company
- **Contacts Per Company**: 3-5 key decision-makers

## Tiering Strategy

### Tier 1 (High Priority)
- Large financial advisory firms with multiple advisers
- Firms with technology/operations leadership roles
- Companies showing growth/scaling indicators

### Tier 2 (Medium Priority)
- Mid-sized advisory firms
- Technology providers with complementary offerings
- Companies with identifiable pain points

### Tier 3 (Nurture)
- Smaller advisory firms
- Media/PR contacts for relationship building
- Early-stage or peripheral prospects

| Week | Focus                             | Companies    | Actions                         |

| 4    | Tier 2 - Second batch + Follow-up | 10 companies | New deployments + Week 3 follow-ups |

## Omni-Channel Sequence Structure

### Day 1: Initial Outreach
- **Email**: Personalized introduction referencing EATT event
- **LinkedIn**: Connection request with personalized note

### Day 3: Value-Add Follow-Up
- **Email**: Share relevant content/case study (if no response)
- **LinkedIn**: Engage with prospect's content if connected

### Day 7: Problem-Solution Framing
- **Email**: Focused on specific pain points identified at EATT
- **LinkedIn Message**: Direct message if connected (different angle from email)

### Day 10: Social Proof
- **Email**: Customer success story relevant to their segment
- **LinkedIn**: Share relevant content and tag prospect if connected

### Day 15: Final Outreach
- **Email**: Final value proposition with clear CTA
- **LinkedIn**: Final personalized outreach if not yet engaged

## Tier-Specific Email Templates

### Tier 1 Templates (High Priority - Enterprise Focus)
- **Subject Line**: "{Company} + Automwrite: Scaling Report Writing Without Adding Headcount"
- **Key Messaging**: Scale, efficiency, team empowerment, compliance confidence
- **CTAs**: Book a discovery call, personalized demo with sample report generation
- **Personas**: Scaling Firm Sam, Wealth-Delegator William, Adviser Network Founder Alastair

### Tier 2 Templates (Mid-Market Focus)
- **Subject Line**: "Report Writing Automation for {Company}'s Growth Journey"
- **Key Messaging**: Growth enablement, competitive advantage, time savings
- **CTAs**: 30-minute demo, free trial offer
- **Personas**: Efficiency-Oriented Ethan, Growth-Oriented Olivia

### Tier 3 Templates (Relationship Building)
- **Subject Line**: "Thought You'd Find This Interesting, {First Name}"
- **Key Messaging**: ROI, ease of implementation, success stories from similar firms
- **CTAs**: Content downloads, webinar invitation, lightweight engagement
- **Personas**: Independent Adviser Emma, Cost-Conscious Chris, Media relationships
