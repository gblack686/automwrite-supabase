# Sales Pipeline Summary - June 10th

This document outlines a comprehensive sales pipeline strategy, covering lead generation, sales tactics, and performance metrics. All actionable items are tracked as recurring tasks in Microsoft Planner, with individuals responsible for weekly updates.

## 1. Lead Generation

### Automated Outreach
- **Contact Source:** 3.5k contacts from Clay and a 7k-firm CSV.
- **LinkedIn Strategy:**
  - **Volume:** ~1,612 connection requests and 150 InMails per month.
  - **Expected Results:** 14 warm leads for sales and 5 automated onboardings monthly.
- **Email Strategy:**
  - **Volume:** ~9,000 outbound emails per month.
  - **Expected Results:** 12 automated onboardings monthly, with ~1,200 prospects added to the newsletter as semi-warm leads.
- **Weekly Actionable:** Monitor and report on connection/reply rates and leads generated.

### Marketing Outreach
- **Top-of-Funnel (Awareness & Interest):**
  - **Google Ads:** £1,250/mo budget to generate 12-15 SQLs.
  - **Meta/Instagram Ads:** £400/mo budget focused on building a retargeting pool.
  - **LinkedIn Ads:** £700/mo budget for prospecting and retargeting, aiming for +500 followers/quarter.
  - **Organic Social:** 12 posts/month across platforms with a target of 8% engagement on LinkedIn.
- **Mid-funnel (Engagement & Consideration):**
  - **SEO/Blog:** 4 long-tail keyword-focused posts per month.
  - **Newsletter:** Monthly emails to trial users and warm leads, targeting a 25% open rate.
- **Bottom-of-funnel (Conversion & Retention):**
  - A/B testing on landing pages to increase form submissions by 15% by Q4.
  - Automated onboarding email sequences.
  - Aim to keep monthly churn below 2%.
- **Weekly Actionable:** Report on ad performance (target <£150 cost-per-onboard), content engagement, and lead progression.

## 2. Sales Strategy
The sales approach is tiered based on the size of the target advisory firm, referred to as "DLCs".

- **DLC1 (Current Focus):** Firms with 3-5 advisors. Involves 20-minute sales calls/demos.
- **DLC2:** Firms with 10-15 advisors. Requires 30-minute calls.
- **DLC3:** Firms with 15+ advisors. Utilizes a multi-step sales process, including in-person meetings.

**Core Rule:** Always call first, then email.

### Conversion Process
- Leads from automated and marketing outreach are categorized as "cold" or "warm".
- The sales team makes ~25 calls daily, prioritizing warm leads.
- **Weekly Actionable:** Report on leads received, calls made, meetings booked, demos, conversion rates, and the current DLC focus.

## 3. Monthly Objectives & Metrics

### Automated Outreach Metrics
- **Volume:** 1,600+ LinkedIn connections and 9,000+ emails per month.
- **Conversion:** Maintain ~4% reply rate on LinkedIn and ~2% on email, leading to a combined ~17 automated onboardings per month.

### Marketing Outreach Metrics
- **ROAS:** Drive Google Ads cost-per-demo towards £150.
- **Growth:** Increase organic sessions by 10% MoM and achieve +500 LinkedIn followers per quarter.
- **Engagement:** Target 8% on LinkedIn posts and 25% open / 3% click rates for the newsletter.

### Sales Metrics
- **Activity:** 500-550 calls per month.
- **Pipeline:** Track meetings booked, demos completed, and win/loss rates.
- **Conversion:** Monitor demo-to-client conversion rate.
